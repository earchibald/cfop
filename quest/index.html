In the headset

  • Your hands do it all, so you keep the cube in your hands. One free hand is enough.
  • Poke a button under the cube with a fingertip: Tilt, Level, Clear, Hidden faces, Smaller, Larger, Hide panel.
  • The row under them sets the hold: Top white or yellow, Front green, orange, blue or red.
  • Exit is at the end of that row. Poke it, then poke it again to come back to this page.
  • Drill mode, in the third row, shows a case on the picture (Sune the first time), whatever your cube shows. Your turns turn it. Solve it and the case comes again. Stop drill shows your own cube again.
  • New scramble, in the third row too, puts a random scramble on the picture the same way. Solve it and another comes. Real cube state shows your own cube again, as it is now.
  • In Drill mode, say a case name to drill it: any two-look OLL or PLL case, or any PLL, such as "T perm", "Sune", "headlights" or "U a perm". Say "again" for the case again, "stop drill" to end. Say the hold as on the Smart cube tab, such as "yellow up", "green front" or "top yellow, front green", or turn it with "x prime" or "turn y". Say "clear moves", or "reset" for the case again. The panel shows what was heard. Start Drill mode on this page once first, so the headset asks for the microphone here.
  • Pinch the cube to carry it. Hold a pinch away from it for a moment to bring it back in front of you.
  • With controllers: grip near the cube carries it, the trigger away brings it back. A: tilt. B: level. Right stick: size; press it: hidden faces. X: clear. Y: panel. Press the left stick: exit.
