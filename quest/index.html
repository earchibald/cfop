In the headset

  • Your hands do it all, so you keep the cube in your hands. One free hand is enough.
  • Poke a button under the cube with a fingertip: Tilt, Level, Clear, Smaller, Larger, Hide panel.
  • Pinch the cube to carry it. Hold a pinch away from it for a moment to bring it back in front of you.
  • With controllers: grip near the cube carries it, the trigger away brings it back. A: tilt. B: level. Right stick: size. X: clear. Y: panel.

For a test on a computer: the keys U, R, F, D, L and B turn the cube when no smart cube is connected. Shift turns it the other way.
