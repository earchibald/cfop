The cube reports its tilt, not a hit. A smack is a short jolt that comes back. A push on a face turns the cube about an axis across that face, so a smack on the right face, or the left, keeps the jolt out of the left-right axis. The tilt alone cannot tell right from left.

Turn the whole cube

Turn with the tilt, in the bar, turns this on. The log says when a turn starts, how it goes on and when it is over.

turn–

A turn that comes to rest within the snap of another hold goes on the list, dashed, and the hold follows it. One that rests between holds, or back at the hold, changes nothing. Reset cube starts the rest pose over.

Reset with twists

R R' R R' R R', or U' U U' U U' U: six quarter turns of one face, each one undone by the next. They leave the cube, and the list, as they were. Another face, a half turn, or the same turn twice starts the count over. The lab waits the time set, then does Reset cube.
